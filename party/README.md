# It's Party Time!

You're going to run a personal security party, amazing! If you do this right, you're doing your friends and family a great service.

And it can be fun! Pick a theme (Shrekurity in the swamp, anyone?), make it a potluck, or put on a bad Christmas movie while you work.

This directory contains all the resources you'll need to run the party. Feel free to make it your own, and add or remove items as you see fit (within security best practices). Even if you don't feel like you're a technical expert, you should still give it a try - just being the one to bring people together to try to get this done as a group is far, far better than doing nothing.

## What Are We Doing?

The idea is simple: one person (that's you!) gathers a group of friends to sit down together and get their personal security sorted out. This is a serious undertaking, because you'll be dealing with sensitive passwords and accounts. So do it at home, with a group of people who trust each other (or trust that you won't invite somebody sketchy to the party). The facilitator guidelines have rules of engagement that help keep everyone safe, and it's your job as host to make sure everyone follows them.

## Was It a Good Party?

You should consider your party a success if participants:
* Leave with a clearer understanding of the types of threats they should look out for.
* Have taken a concrete step towards better personal security, like setting up two-factor login on key accounts.
* Know what's left to finish their basic setup from the party (you don't have to finish everything!).

You should consider your party an ultra super success if your friends go on to run their own party, or help their parents set up protocols to protect against scams.
