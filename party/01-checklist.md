# Facilitator Checklist

This checklist covers what to do before, during and after the party.

## A Few Days Before

- [ ] Only invite people who know and trust each other.
- [ ] Send participants [Before the Party](participants/01-prepare.md), which includes the rules of engagement.
- [ ] Pick one or two password managers to recommend, and set one up yourself so that you know the steps.
- [ ] Read through the [party checklist](participants/02-checklist.md) on an iPhone and an Android phone, if you can borrow one.
- [ ] If the group is large, ask another technical friend to help (and make sure they know the rules of engagement).
- [ ] Check that the venue has reliable wifi and enough power outlets (or bring an extension cord).
- [ ] Set up seating so that people aren't looking straight at each other's screens.

## Opening

- [ ] Give the opening talk, using the [talk notes](02-talk-notes.md).
- [ ] Go through the [rules of engagement](README.md#rules-of-engagement), and check that everyone agrees to them.
- [ ] Share the [party checklist](participants/02-checklist.md).
- [ ] Remind everyone to save recovery codes *before* turning on two-factor login.

## During

- [ ] Move around the room and check on progress, without looking at screens while people type passwords or codes.
- [ ] Help by pointing and explaining, not by taking devices.
- [ ] Watch for anyone about to change a password or turn on two-factor login without recovery details in place.
- [ ] Gently call out anyone breaking the rules of engagement.
- [ ] Make sure everyone gets through at least their password manager and first passwords.

## Wrap Up

- [ ] Ask everyone to fill in the follow-up section of their checklist.
- [ ] Remind everyone to take their recovery codes home, and to change any password they think someone else may have seen.
- [ ] Share [After the Party](participants/03-take-home.md) and [Protecting Your Parents](participants/04-parents.md).
- [ ] Ask what worked and what didn't, and write it down.

## After

- [ ] Check in with participants a week or two later about their follow-ups.
- [ ] Update these materials with what you learned (or open an issue).
