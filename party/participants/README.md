# Security Party: Participant Documents

These are the documents for participants at a security party.

Getting together to do this means handling sensitive information around other people, so please read the rules of engagement in [Before the Party](01-prepare.md) before you arrive.

1. [Before the Party](01-prepare.md): what to bring, what to do beforehand, and how we'll keep each other safe.
2. [Party Checklist](02-checklist.md): what we'll work through together at the party.
3. [After the Party](03-take-home.md): what to keep in mind, regular maintenance, and what to do if something goes wrong.
4. [Protecting Your Parents](04-parents.md): rules to agree on with your parents to protect them from scams.
5. [Resources](05-resources.md): short, approachable reading from trusted security folks.
