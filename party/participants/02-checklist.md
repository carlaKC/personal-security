# Party Checklist

This checklist gets you to a good starting state. Work through it in order, since each section builds on the one before, and ask for help if you get stuck.

## 1. Your Phone

- [ ] Your phone is up to date, and automatic updates are on.
- [ ] It has a passcode of at least six digits, plus Face ID or fingerprint unlock.
- [ ] Theft protection is on (iPhone: Stolen Device Protection, Android: Theft protection).
- [ ] Find My (iPhone) or Find My Device (Android) is on.
- [ ] Backups are on (iCloud Backup or Google backup).

## 2. Your Apple or Google Account

- [ ] Your recovery email and phone number are correct.
- [ ] You have a way back in if you lose your phone (recovery codes on paper, or a recovery contact on iPhone).
- [ ] Two-factor login is on.

## 3. Your Password Manager

- [ ] You've picked a password manager and installed it on your phone (and laptop).
- [ ] Its main password is a long passphrase (four or more random words) that you don't use anywhere else.
- [ ] Two-factor login is on.
- [ ] Its recovery details are written down, to keep somewhere safe at home.

## 4. Your First Passwords

- [ ] Your main email has a new, generated password saved in your password manager.
- [ ] Two-factor login is on for your email, using an authenticator app or passkey if possible (text message codes are better than nothing).
- [ ] One more important account, like your bank, is saved in your password manager.

## Optional

- [ ] Check your email address on haveibeenpwned.com to see which data breaches it has appeared in. This helps you decide which passwords to change first.

## Follow Up Later

You don't need to do everything today. Note what you'll come back to:

- [ ] Move the rest of my passwords into my password manager (easiest done one at a time, as I log in to each account).
- [ ] Turn on two-factor login for: ________________________
- [ ] Ask my phone carrier for a PIN or lock that stops my number being moved to another SIM.
- [ ] Set up a safe word and scam rules with my parents (see the parents guide).
