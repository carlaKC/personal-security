# Before the Party

This page covers what to bring and what to do before the party. Most of the time at these events is lost to slow updates and forgotten passwords, so doing this ahead of time leaves the evening for the things that matter.

## Bring

- Your phone and charger.
- Your laptop and charger, if you have one.
- A pen and paper, for recovery codes that you'll keep at home.

## Do Beforehand

1. Check that you can sign in to your Apple ID (or Google account on Android) and your main email. If you can't, reset the password at home.
2. Update your phone and laptop. (iPhone: Settings > General > Software Update. Android: Settings > System > Software update.)
3. Write a list of your most important accounts: email, bank, phone carrier and any social accounts you'd hate to lose. Names only, not passwords.

## Rules of Engagement

Sorting out your security in a room with other people means passwords and recovery codes will be close at hand. We're doing this among friends who trust each other, but we'll still be careful:
1. Don't look at anyone else's screen, and keep yours to yourself while you type passwords or codes.
2. Don't touch anyone else's device. Helpers will point and explain.
3. Never say passwords or codes out loud, or write them down for someone else.
4. Keep recovery codes face down, and take them home.
5. No photos or screen recordings.
6. Lock your phone when you put it down.
7. Anyone can skip a step and do it at home instead, no questions asked.

You don't need to pick a password manager in advance, we'll do that together. And you don't need to be good with tech - that's the point of the party.
